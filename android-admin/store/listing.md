# Google Play listing: ScreenTinker Manager

Paste into Play Console → Store presence → Main store listing. Limits: name 30, short 80, full 4000.

## App name

ScreenTinker Manager

## Short description

Update what your ScreenTinker screens show, right from your phone.

## Full description

ScreenTinker Manager is the easy way to keep your digital signage up to date from your phone.

Snap a photo of today's special, pick it in the app, choose the screens, and it is showing within seconds. No laptop, no training.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Add a screen: type the 6-digit code shown on the TV
• Change what a screen shows: replace it, add to it, or show the same as another screen
• Upload photos and videos straight from your gallery, or share them from any app
• Pick from photos you uploaded before
• Invite a colleague to help look after your screens
• Switch between several ScreenTinker servers

WORKS WITH YOUR SERVER
Use it with screentinker.com or with your own self-hosted ScreenTinker server, including servers on your local network. Sign in with email and password or with your organization's single sign-on (Google, Microsoft and other providers your server offers).

If your workspace has content review turned on, the app tells you your changes were sent for review, never that they are already showing.

Need schedules, layouts, widgets or video walls? Tap "Advanced" to open the full dashboard in your browser.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
The app talks only to the server you choose. No ads, no tracking, no analytics.

FREE AND OPEN SOURCE
ScreenTinker is open source: https://github.com/screentinker
If it is useful to you, you can support its development through GitHub Sponsors: https://github.com/sponsors/screentinker

The screens themselves run the free ScreenTinker player, available for Android TV, Fire TV, Roku, LG, Samsung, Raspberry Pi, Windows, ChromeOS and any web browser.
